# Exhibit playtest kit

Use this kit to watch people try the exhibit and to write down what happens. No playtest has taken
place yet. Nothing in this repository reports one.

## Ground rules

- **Consent.** Ask before you watch. "We are testing a library exhibit, not you. May I watch and
  take notes? No names, no photos, no recordings." If the person says no, thank them and step away.
- **No personal data.** Write no names, ages or descriptions of people, and take no photos, audio
  or video. Write only what happened on the screen and what was said about the exhibit.
- **Hands off.** Do not explain the exhibit before someone tries it. Answer a question only if the
  visitor is stuck and asks, and write down that you helped.
- **Care.** If anyone is upset by a page, stop the session, thank them, and write "stopped" with
  no further detail. Report the round id to the exhibit's author the same day.

## Protocol for one session (5 to 10 minutes)

1. Reset the kiosk to the attract screen by waiting a minute, or by touching Home.
2. Start the timer when the visitor first touches the screen or the dial.
3. Watch without speaking. Fill in the observation sheet as you go.
4. When they leave, or after 5 minutes, ask the three questions at the end of the sheet. Write the
   answers in their own words, as close as you can.
5. Stop the timer. One sheet per session.

## Summarizing a playtest day

Count sessions, the minutes for each, the modes played, rounds finished, and how many people found
the start without help. Quote answers to the three questions word for word. Report problems by
round id. Write down the number of observers and the day and place. Never report a percentage from
fewer than ten sessions without also giving the count.
